The route here

Same job, different tools

The titles changed. The actual work — learn the system, then explain it to the next person — mostly didn’t.

  1. Cabling, out of high school

    Ethernet, coax and fiber, before I fully knew what any of it was for.

  2. Army — satellite communications

    Satcom was my specialty, alongside operating systems and routing/switching for both data and voice.

  3. Contracting, into software-defined radios

    More than two decades in, still chasing the same signal. By the end it was software-defined radios — cell-phone-sized computers with extra antennas, and very different apps.

  4. A detour through crypto

    Did well enough to walk away from contracting entirely — then learned fast that a number on a screen isn’t the same as a role. Spent a stretch in the crypto social scene just getting by.

  5. Prime Inc.

    Trucking showed up next, and it fit: I’d always ended up being the support engineer or instructor in the room or field, and I’d always loved the road. Now I do both for a living.
