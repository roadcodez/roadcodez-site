How it fits together

The road feeds the lab.

  1. Drive

    The road shows what actually breaks: routing, parking, paperwork, fatigue, the gap between the plan and the pavement.

  2. Teach

    What works gets turned into repeatable training, so the next driver learns it in a lot instead of on a bad day.

  3. Automate

    AI takes the busywork off drivers and small operators — privately, on their own terms, with a human in charge.
