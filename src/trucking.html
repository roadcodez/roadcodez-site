Field notes

Lessons from the right seat

Truck GPS is not car GPS

A 13 ft 6 in trailer needs routing that knows about bridge clearances, weight limits and truck-restricted roads. Ordinary car navigation doesn’t — and the low bridge doesn’t care what your phone said.

Hours of service in one breath

Inside a 14-hour on-duty window you can drive up to 11 hours, and a 30-minute break is required after 8 hours of driving. Sleeper-berth splits add flexibility. The rules get revised, so check current FMCSA guidance.

Chains and super-single tires

Many drive axles now run super-single tires, which need chains made for that setup — standard dual-tire chain sets won’t fit. Know the chain requirements before the pass, not at it.

Parking is part of the plan

A 53-foot trailer limits where you can stop. Plan your parking and your hours together, because the legal place to run out of hours is rarely where you want to be.
