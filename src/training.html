Backing, simplified

Three patterns to master

Almost every dock, lot and truck stop comes down to one of these three.

How a session runs

Show it. Say it. Do it. Review it.

  1. Get out and look

    Walk the space. Know what’s behind, beside and above before moving an inch.

  2. Say the plan

    Name the pivot point and the path out loud. A plan you can say is a plan you can fix.

  3. Do the reps

    Repeat until the set-up is automatic. Pull up whenever it goes sideways — that’s a skill, not a failure.

  4. Debrief

    One honest takeaway per attempt: what worked, what changes next time.
