Legal · Privacy

Privacy Statement

Short version: we don’t sell, rent, or hand your name and contact details to anyone. Longer version below.

Last updated: November 8, 2026. Plain-language statement, not a substitute for legal advice.

Who this covers

This statement covers roadcodez.com, operated by RoadCodez LLC (Wyoming). Contact: Matt (“Ogre”) at matt@roadcodez.com. Trucking and training content on this site reflects his personal experience as an independent contractor for Prime Inc. — this is not a Prime Inc. privacy policy and doesn’t cover anything Prime does with your information.

What we actually collect today

  • No accounts, no sign-ups, no cookies set by this site.
  • No on-site tracking or analytics are running right now.
  • The “Email” buttons are plain mailto links — they open your own email app. Your message goes directly from you to Matt’s inbox through your own email provider; this site never sees or stores it.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub’s infrastructure logs standard technical request data (like IP address and browser type) the way any web host does, under GitHub’s own privacy statement — RoadCodez doesn’t separately collect or have access to that beyond standard aggregate traffic reporting.

What we might add later

  • Basic analytics — aggregate numbers like page views, referring sites and device type, to understand traffic. Not tied to your name.
  • An on-site contact form — if one ever replaces or supplements the mailto links, whatever you type into it (name, email, message) is used only to answer you, same as an email would be.

If either of these goes live, this page gets updated first, with the date above changed.

What we never do

We don’t sell, rent, trade or otherwise hand your name, email, or anything tied to your identity to any third party, for marketing or anything else. The only exception is aggregate, de-identified statistics — the kind that says “most visitors look at the Training page first,” with no way to trace it back to any individual — which we may compile or share.

Legal requests

We comply with valid legal process. Where a request for user data looks overbroad, unnecessary, or inconsistent with the trust you’ve placed in us, we’ll push back through every lawful means available before producing anything — and we’ll tell you about it, unless the law specifically forbids that.

Your rights, kids, and changes

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you — which today is essentially whatever you’ve emailed us. Just ask; we’ll handle it directly, not through a ticket queue.

This site isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

We may update this statement as the site changes. Material changes will be noted here with a new date.
